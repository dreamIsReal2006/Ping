Ping Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Ping is an offline rhythm game. The app does not require an account or transmit gameplay scores to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Ping stores your high score and game settings locally on your device so they remain available when you reopen the app. This information is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information.

Permissions

The game can use your device's vibration feature for optional gameplay feedback. The app does not request notification permission and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Children's privacy

Ping does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes and contact

This policy must be updated if the app's data practices change. For questions about this policy, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the Ping Google Play listing.
